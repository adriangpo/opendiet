# FR-011: Save Open Food Facts Product

## Requirement

**ID:** FR-011
**Title:** Save Open Food Facts Product
**Priority:** Must Have
**Status:** Draft

### Statement

OpenDiet shall allow the user to save an Open Food Facts product into the local catalog for offline reuse.

## Traceability

| Traces To | ID | Description |
|-----------|-----|-------------|
| Customer Need | CN-007 | Obtain and reuse food data from Open Food Facts |
| Customer Problem | CP-005 | The user expects to log the foods they actually eat |
| Customer Problem | CP-002 | Logging must work offline once a food is saved |

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] A saved product is available for logging without a further network call.
- [ ] Saved nutrition data preserves the fields provided by Open Food Facts, including micronutrients when present.
- [ ] A saved product can be edited locally without altering the Open Food Facts source.
