# FR-007: Ungated Access

## Requirement

**ID:** FR-007
**Title:** Ungated Access
**Priority:** Must Have
**Status:** Draft

### Statement

OpenDiet shall make all v1 capabilities available to the user without payment, mandatory account creation, or advertising.

## Traceability

| Traces To | ID | Description |
|-----------|-----|-------------|
| Customer Need | CN-005 | Provide the means to log and track fully, with no payment or ads |
| Customer Problem | CP-004 | The user expects core capabilities free of paywalls and ads |

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] No v1 feature is locked behind a purchase or subscription.
- [ ] The app is usable without creating any account (the Open Food Facts account is optional and only needed to contribute).
- [ ] No advertising is presented anywhere in the app.
